Finding your mover shouldn’t feel like moving

A 2026 redesign of moveBuddha’s mover profile, the page where people decide whether to contact a moving company. It’s currently in an A/B test against the live page.

moveBuddha

Before asking for a quote, the page has to answer two things: can I trust this mover, and what will it cost?

Three questions on the way to a quote

Lands on a mover profileRequests a quote
Question 1Can I trust them?
BeforeRating and complaints buried in paragraphs
AfterSub-scores, top praise and top complaint at a glance
Question 2What will it cost?
BeforeNo price until far down the page
AfterState average hourly rate right above the button
Question 3What do I do next?
BeforeScore on one side, button on the other
AfterOne panel: quote first, call second

The quote button never leaves reach

On a phone it comes right after the evidence. On desktop it lives in a sticky column, one click away at any scroll depth.

Calling, without surprises

Call offers to save the contact first, so people recognize the mover when they call back.

Price before the ask, across the site

Route pages open with a cost estimate. Price insights show the cheapest month to move.
